01 — Who I am

Designing with intention
and curiosity.

SD

Hi, I'm Somya Dang — a second-year Bachelor of Design (UI/UX) student with a deep love for crafting digital experiences that feel genuinely human.

My work lives at the intersection of empathy and aesthetics. I believe great design isn't just about how something looks — it's about how it makes someone feel when they use it.

When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm sketching in my journal, exploring creative code, or hunting for the perfect chai.
